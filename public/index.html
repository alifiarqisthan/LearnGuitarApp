HOW TO LEARN A SONG

One section at a time

  1. Look at the chords and play them slowly.
  2. Hum the melody or count the beat.
  3. Hide the chord names and try from memory.
  4. Reveal the chart and choose what to review.
REMEMBER

Your version is yours

You can transpose the song to a comfortable key, change the chord chart, and add practice notes. The goal is to make music, not to get everything perfect on the first try.
